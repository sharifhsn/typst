    A practical path to better collaboration

    Typst gives authors a fast, programmable writing environment while Word remains the lingua franca of academic review.

    Our central claim is round trips should preserve intent, not merely copy visible text.

    What survives the trip

    • Headings, paragraphs, lists, links, captions, and table cells
    • Comments, tracked edits, headers, footers, and footnotes
